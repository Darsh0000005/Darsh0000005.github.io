FlipHTML5 to PDF

Turn a FlipHTML5 flipbook into a PDF

Paste the book’s link and your PDF downloads when it’s ready. Pages travel straight from FlipHTML5 to your browser and are put together on your device. Nothing is uploaded to this site.

Paste several links, separated by spaces or new lines, to queue them all.

For personal, educational use. Only convert books you’re entitled to copy, and respect each publisher’s rights. Books marked private or protected are not converted.

Your conversions

    How it works

    1. Paste a link. Open the book on fliphtml5.com and copy the address from your browser’s address bar.
    2. Pages are collected. Your browser downloads each page image directly from FlipHTML5, a few at a time.
    3. The PDF is built here. The pages are stitched into a PDF on your device and saved to your downloads.
    What does a valid link look like?

    Something like https://fliphtml5.com/abcd/efgh/ or https://online.fliphtml5.com/abcd/efgh/. The two short codes after the domain identify the book; anything after them is ignored.

    Is anything uploaded or stored?

    No. This site is just a web page. Downloads go from FlipHTML5 to your browser, and the PDF is created in your browser’s memory. There are no accounts, no database and no server that sees your books.

    Why won’t some books convert?

    Books their publisher marked as private or protected are refused on purpose, and so are books that were removed or need a password. Very large books (over 2,000 pages) are also refused because a browser can’t safely hold them all at once.

    Why are there blank pages in my PDF?

    A page that still can’t be downloaded after a few attempts is replaced with a blank page, and the card tells you which ones. Use Convert again to fill them in.

    Can I select or search the text?

    No. FlipHTML5 serves each page as a picture, so the PDF is made of page images without a text layer.
