How it works

  1. 01

    Copy the link

    Open the book on FlipHTML5 and copy the link from your browser's address bar.

  2. 02

    Paste and choose

    Pick a quality. Turn on Selectable text if you want to copy or search the words in your PDF.

  3. 03

    Get your PDF

    Keep the tab open while the pages load. The PDF saves to your downloads when every page is done.

Questions

Why can't I select text in my PDF?

FlipHTML5 stores most books as pictures of pages, so there's no text to copy. Turn on Selectable text (OCR) and the site reads the words and adds an invisible text layer over each page. You can then select, copy and search, and the pages look exactly the same.

How accurate is the text?

Very good on clean printed pages. It can get the odd letter wrong, and it may miss stylised headings or text inside photos.

Why did my book fail?

Books the publisher marked as private or encrypted can't be downloaded. Check that the link opens the book on FlipHTML5, then try again.

Do I need to upload anything?

No. There's no account and no upload. Your PDF is put together in your browser, and OCR runs on your device.
